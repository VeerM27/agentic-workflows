# Industry: Fintech & personal finance

**Audience:** Consumers managing money, and small businesses; they care about safety, clarity and control.
**Tone of voice:** Calm, precise, plain-spoken. Never hype returns.
**Typical pages / features:** Dashboard with balances, transactions list with search/filters, transfers/payments flow, cards, insights, security settings, onboarding/KYC.
**Visual conventions:** Clear data hierarchy, tabular numerals, generous whitespace around figures, obvious confirmation steps for money movement.
**Palette tendencies:** Deep blues/greens or confident neutrals with one vivid accent; reserve red strictly for losses/errors and green for gains.
**Trust & compliance:** Show fees and terms clearly; regulatory disclaimers in footers; strong security cues (2FA, masked data); never imply guaranteed returns.
**Avoid:** Stock-photo handshakes, crypto-neon clichés, dark patterns around consent or cancellation.
