# Industry: Legal & professional services

**Audience:** Individuals and businesses seeking expertise they can trust.
**Tone of voice:** Authoritative, clear, human; avoid jargon.
**Typical pages / features:** Practice areas, team/profiles, case studies or insights, contact/consultation booking, fees & process explainer.
**Visual conventions:** Restrained, editorial layouts, strong typography, credible people photography.
**Palette tendencies:** Deep navy, burgundy, charcoal, with refined neutrals and a subtle metallic accent.
**Trust & compliance:** Regulatory disclaimers (no legal advice / attorney advertising), clear complaints procedure, confidentiality messaging.
**Avoid:** Gavel and scales clichés, dense jargon, no visible way to get in touch.
