# Industry: E-commerce & retail

**Audience:** Shoppers comparing products, mostly on mobile, with little patience.
**Tone of voice:** Brand-led; clear and persuasive without pressure.
**Typical pages / features:** Home/merchandising, category listing with filters, product detail (gallery, variants, reviews), cart, checkout, account/orders, search.
**Visual conventions:** Product imagery first, fast scanning grids, prominent price and add-to-cart, trust signals near purchase.
**Palette tendencies:** Neutral canvas that lets products shine, brand accent for actions; sale colours used sparingly.
**Trust & compliance:** Clear returns, shipping and pricing (incl. taxes); accessible forms; no fake urgency or hidden costs.
**Avoid:** Fake countdown timers, cluttered pop-ups, tiny product photos.
