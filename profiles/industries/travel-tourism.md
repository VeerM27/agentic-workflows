# Industry: Travel & tourism

**Audience:** Travellers dreaming, planning and booking, often on mobile.
**Tone of voice:** Evocative, helpful, trustworthy.
**Typical pages / features:** Destination pages, search/booking flow, itineraries, deals, reviews, maps, travel info.
**Visual conventions:** Immersive photography, maps, clear booking path, social proof.
**Palette tendencies:** Drawn from the destinations: sea blues, sand, lush greens, sunset warm tones.
**Trust & compliance:** Clear total pricing, cancellation terms, accessibility information for venues.
**Avoid:** Hidden fees, cluttered deal grids, slow image-heavy pages.
