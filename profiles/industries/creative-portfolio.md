# Industry: Creative studios & portfolios

**Audience:** Clients and collaborators judging taste and craft.
**Tone of voice:** Confident, personal, concise.
**Typical pages / features:** Work index, case study pages, about/process, services, contact.
**Visual conventions:** The work is the hero: bold layouts, art direction, expressive typography and motion (scroll-driven reveals suit this well).
**Palette tendencies:** Highly individual: monochrome with one accent, or a signature palette drawn from the studio's identity.
**Trust & compliance:** Credit collaborators, image alt text, performance with heavy media.
**Avoid:** Template portfolio grids with no point of view, motion that blocks content.
