# Industry: Education & e-learning

**Audience:** Learners (from children to professionals), parents, teachers.
**Tone of voice:** Encouraging, clear, structured.
**Typical pages / features:** Course catalogue, course page (syllabus, outcomes, instructor), lesson player, progress dashboard, enrolment/pricing, admissions info.
**Visual conventions:** Clear structure and progress cues, readable long-form content, friendly illustration.
**Palette tendencies:** Bright but balanced palettes for younger audiences; trustworthy deep tones for institutions.
**Trust & compliance:** Accessibility for learners with disabilities (captions, transcripts), child privacy (COPPA-style) where relevant.
**Avoid:** Walls of text, unclear pricing, gamification that distracts from learning.
