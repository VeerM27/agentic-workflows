# Industry: Fitness & wellness

**Audience:** People building habits: gym members, class-goers, app users.
**Tone of voice:** Energetic or calm depending on the brand (HIIT vs. yoga); always encouraging, never shaming.
**Typical pages / features:** Class schedule & booking, memberships/pricing, trainers, programmes, progress tracking, community/challenges.
**Visual conventions:** Motion and vitality, strong photography of real bodies, clear schedules, progress visualisation.
**Palette tendencies:** Energetic: bold contrasts and vivid accents. Calm: earthy, soft and natural tones.
**Trust & compliance:** Health disclaimers, inclusive imagery and language, accessible schedules.
**Avoid:** Body shaming, unrealistic before/after imagery, aggressive upsells.
