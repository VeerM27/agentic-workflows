# Industry: Healthcare & clinics

**Audience:** Patients (often anxious, sometimes older or with impairments) and clinicians.
**Tone of voice:** Warm, reassuring, plain language (aim for a reading age of 11–12).
**Typical pages / features:** Find a clinician/service, booking flow, patient portal (appointments, results, messages), service pages, locations & hours, insurance info.
**Visual conventions:** Large tap targets, high legibility, clear wayfinding, calm layouts; people-first imagery with diversity.
**Palette tendencies:** Soft blues, teals and greens with warm neutrals; avoid harsh reds except for urgent alerts.
**Trust & compliance:** Privacy (HIPAA/GDPR-style) messaging, emergency disclaimer ('call emergency services'), WCAG AA strictly, no medical claims without sources.
**Avoid:** Clinical coldness, tiny text, hidden phone numbers, sensational health claims.
