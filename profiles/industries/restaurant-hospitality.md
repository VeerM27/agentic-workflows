# Industry: Restaurants, cafés & hospitality

**Audience:** Locals and visitors deciding where to eat or stay, mostly on phones.
**Tone of voice:** Inviting and characterful; match the venue's personality.
**Typical pages / features:** Home with atmosphere, menu (readable HTML, not PDF), reservations/booking, location & hours, gallery, events, gift cards, ordering.
**Visual conventions:** Appetising photography, strong brand personality, menus that scan quickly on mobile, sticky 'Book a table' action.
**Palette tendencies:** Drawn from the cuisine and interior: warm terracottas, deep greens, cream, charcoal; avoid clinical blues.
**Trust & compliance:** Allergen and dietary labelling, accessible menus, clear opening hours and pricing.
**Avoid:** PDF menus, autoplay music, burying the address and hours.
