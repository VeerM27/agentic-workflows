# Industry: Nonprofits & charities

**Audience:** Donors, volunteers and the communities served.
**Tone of voice:** Hopeful, human, urgent but never manipulative.
**Typical pages / features:** Mission/impact, programmes, stories, donate flow (one-off/monthly), volunteer, events, transparency/reports.
**Visual conventions:** Real people and stories, impact numbers, prominent but respectful donate actions.
**Palette tendencies:** Warm, hopeful brand colours with strong contrast for calls to action.
**Trust & compliance:** Financial transparency, dignified representation of beneficiaries, accessible donation forms, data protection.
**Avoid:** Poverty imagery that strips dignity, guilt-driven copy, long donation forms.
