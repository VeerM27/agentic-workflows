# Industry: B2B SaaS

**Audience:** Busy professionals and buyers evaluating tools; they want proof and clarity fast.
**Tone of voice:** Confident, specific, benefit-led; avoid buzzwords.
**Typical pages / features:** Landing page, product/features, pricing with plan comparison, customers/case studies, docs, sign-up/onboarding, app dashboard and settings.
**Visual conventions:** Clear product screenshots/illustrations, strong information hierarchy, scannable comparison tables, consistent component system.
**Palette tendencies:** A distinctive brand hue plus disciplined neutrals; ensure dashboards have a calm data palette and semantic status colours.
**Trust & compliance:** Security/compliance badges (SOC 2, GDPR) where true; transparent pricing; accessible data tables.
**Avoid:** Generic purple-gradient hero, meaningless abstract 3D blobs, vague 'all-in-one platform' copy.
