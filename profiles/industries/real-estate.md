# Industry: Real estate & property

**Audience:** Buyers, renters and sellers making high-stakes decisions; agents.
**Tone of voice:** Professional, aspirational but honest.
**Typical pages / features:** Property search with map and filters, listing detail (gallery, floor plan, key facts, mortgage calculator), agent profiles, valuation request, saved searches.
**Visual conventions:** Big photography, clear key facts (price, beds, baths, area), map integration, comparison.
**Palette tendencies:** Sophisticated neutrals with a confident accent (deep navy, forest, brass); avoid loud saturated palettes.
**Trust & compliance:** Fair-housing language, accurate pricing, energy ratings where required.
**Avoid:** Hidden prices, low-quality images, overwhelming filter UIs on mobile.
