# Brief review

## Read the request for
- **Goal**: what should a visitor do or feel after seeing this? If not stated, propose one.
- **Audience**: who is it for, on which devices, in which context.
- **Product facts**: name, key features, materials, price/positioning, claims that need evidence.
- **Experience**: what happens on screen, in what order, and why (for scroll stories: the narrative arc).
- **Content**: headline, supporting copy, calls to action. Missing copy is a gap; propose drafts.
- **Constraints**: brand, tech, timeline, accessibility, legal (claims, trademarks).
- **Success criteria**: how the operator will judge the result.

## Assess the photos
For each image: what it shows, angle, resolution/quality, background (clean cut-out vs. scene),
lighting consistency across images, and how it could be used (hero, detail, texture, colour source).
Flag images that are too small, inconsistent, watermarked or contain people or locations that raise
privacy concerns.

**Resolution rule of thumb:** a photo looks crisp when it is 2–3× wider (in pixels) than the size it is
shown at, because phones and modern laptops have high-density screens. A product shown 560px wide needs a
source view about 1,100–1,700px wide. Report each photo's exact size, and for anything below 2×, say what
it limits (e.g. "hero must stay under 300px wide to look sharp") and ask for a higher-resolution original
as a suggestion, with the fallback that works without one.

## Write useful feedback
- Separate **fixes** (things that would make the result wrong or the work stall) from **suggestions**
  (improvements) and **questions** (only the operator can answer).
- Every point says why it matters for the next agents and what you propose.
- Prefer decisions with stated defaults over open questions; never block on a question.
- Keep the operator's intent and voice. Don't inflate scope: suggest extras as optional.

## The refined brief
Short sections, concrete language, no marketing fluff. Mark added assumptions as **Assumption**.
Include an explicit "Out of scope" list.
